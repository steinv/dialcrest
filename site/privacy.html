Privacy Policy for Dialcrest

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Dialcrest ("the App") is developed by Peblet ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your choices. Dialcrest lets you make and receive calls and send and receive text messages using your own Twilio account and phone number.

Information you provide

Twilio Account SID and Auth Token. When you connect your Twilio account, the App stores your Account SID and Auth Token in your device's secure, encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). They are sent, over encrypted connections, to our backend when it needs to act on your behalf with the Twilio API — for example, to generate a short-lived Twilio access token, register your device for incoming calls, or configure which of your numbers rings the App.

Our backend also keeps a copy of your Auth Token, associated with your Account SID. Twilio signs every call and message notification it sends us with your Auth Token, and we use the stored copy for one purpose only: to verify that those notifications genuinely come from Twilio for your account, so nobody else can impersonate them. We store it only after Twilio has confirmed it is valid for your account, update it when you rotate it, and it can never be read by the App or any other client — only by our backend.

Information collected automatically

Calls and messages. Your call history and text messages are fetched directly from your Twilio account and stored locally on your device so the App can display them. We do not record your calls or keep your message history on our servers. A few things do pass through or briefly stay on our backend:

  • Incoming text messages pass through our backend so it can deliver the notification (sender, recipient and message text) to your devices. They are not stored.
  • Photos and voice notes you send over WhatsApp are uploaded to our storage so Twilio can fetch and deliver them. They are reachable only through an unguessable link and are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Call status updates from Twilio (phone numbers, call status and duration — never call audio) are written to our server logs for troubleshooting and kept for up to 30 days.

The actual calls and SMS/MMS/WhatsApp traffic are carried and billed by Twilio under your own Twilio account and are subject to Twilio's Privacy Policy.

Contacts. With your permission, the App reads your device's contacts to show names instead of raw phone numbers on calls and messages. Contacts are read on-device only and are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere outside your device.

Push notifications. To alert you of incoming calls and messages while the App isn't open, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and Apple Push Notification service / Twilio's VoIP push (iOS). This requires registering a device push token with Twilio and Firebase, which our backend stores associated with your Account SID; this token identifies your device for delivering notifications, not you personally.

Subscription status. The App offers a paid subscription, with a free trial for new subscribers, purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. When you subscribe, your purchase receipt/token is sent to our backend to verify the purchase with Apple or Google. We store your subscription status (plan, expiration date, auto-renew flag, whether you are in the free trial) in our database, keyed by the purchase identifier Apple or Google assigns, together with the Twilio Account SID it was last used with, so the App can check your access level. We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Diagnostic data. We use Firebase App Check to verify that requests to our backend come from a genuine copy of the App. This does not identify you personally.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the App's backend; each processes the data described above only to provide its service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Functions, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, App Check) — hosts our backend, database and file storage, and delivers push notifications.
  • Cloudflare — receives Twilio's call and message notifications for your account, protects them against abuse, and forwards the resulting notifications to your devices.
  • Twilio — carries your calls and messages under your own Twilio account.
  • Apple and Google Play — process subscription purchases and push notifications.

Permissions the App requests

  • Microphone — required to place and receive calls, and to record WhatsApp voice notes.
  • Contacts — required to show contact names for calls and messages (optional; the App works without it, showing phone numbers instead).
  • Notifications — required to alert you of incoming calls and messages.
  • Camera and photos (iOS) — only when you choose to send a photo in a WhatsApp message (optional).

Data retention and deletion

The credentials and call/message cache on your device are deleted when you log out or uninstall the App. On our backend we keep, per Twilio Account SID, your Auth Token, your devices' push tokens, your subscription records, and your number configuration until you ask us to delete them. WhatsApp media is deleted after 7 days and logs after 30 days, as described above. To delete your data from our backend, or for any other privacy request, contact us using the details below. You can also rotate your Auth Token in the Twilio Console at any time, which immediately makes our stored copy useless.

Children's privacy

Dialcrest is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
