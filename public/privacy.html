Privacy Policy for Dialcrest

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Dialcrest ("the App") is developed by Peblet ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your choices. Dialcrest lets you make and receive calls and send and receive text messages using your own Twilio account and phone number.

Information you provide

Twilio Account SID and Auth Token. When you connect your Twilio account, the App stores your Account SID and Auth Token in your device's secure, encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). They are never stored on our servers. They are sent, over encrypted connections, to our backend (Firebase Cloud Functions) only at the moment it's needed to act on your behalf with the Twilio API — for example, to generate a short-lived Twilio access token, register your device for incoming calls, or configure which of your numbers rings the App. Your Auth Token is used transiently for that single request and is not written to our database.

Information collected automatically

Calls and messages. Your call history and text messages are fetched directly from your Twilio account and stored locally on your device so the App can display them. We do not copy or retain the content of your calls or messages on our servers. The actual calls and SMS/MMS traffic are carried and billed by Twilio under your own Twilio account and are subject to Twilio's Privacy Policy.

Contacts. With your permission, the App reads your device's contacts to show names instead of raw phone numbers on calls and messages. Contacts are read on-device only and are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere outside your device.

Push notifications. To alert you of incoming calls and messages while the App isn't open, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and Apple Push Notification service / Twilio's VoIP push (iOS). This requires registering a device push token with Twilio and Firebase; this token identifies your device for delivering notifications, not you personally.

Subscription status. The App offers a free trial followed by a paid subscription, purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. When you subscribe, your purchase receipt/token is sent to our backend to verify the purchase with Apple or Google. We store your subscription status (plan, expiration date, auto-renew flag) associated with your Twilio Account SID in our database, so the App can check your access level. We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Diagnostic data. We use Firebase App Check to verify that requests to our backend come from a genuine copy of the App. This does not identify you personally.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Permissions the App requests

  • Microphone — required to place and receive calls.
  • Contacts — required to show contact names for calls and messages (optional; the App works without it, showing phone numbers instead).
  • Notifications — required to alert you of incoming calls and messages.

Data retention and deletion

Your Twilio credentials and local call/message cache are stored only on your device and are deleted when you log out or uninstall the App. To delete your subscription record from our database, or for any other privacy request, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Dialcrest is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
