Frequently Asked Questions

Dialcrest is a thin, native app in front of your own Twilio account. Most questions below come down to that one idea — here's what it means in practice.

Do I need a Twilio account?

Yes. Dialcrest doesn't provide phone numbers or calling minutes itself — it's an app in front of a Twilio account you own. You'll need a Twilio account with at least one Voice-capable phone number, and your account's Account SID and Auth Token (found on the Twilio Console dashboard).

How do I log in?

On first launch, enter your Twilio Account SID and Auth Token. Dialcrest verifies them with Twilio, then stores them only in your device's encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) — they're never written to our database. From there the app registers your device so it can make and receive calls on your Twilio number.

How do I make a call or send a text?

Use the Dialer tab to type or paste a number — matching contacts are shown automatically — then tap the call button to dial, or switch to the Messages tab to start a text conversation. Whichever number you've set as your Outgoing caller ID (see Settings) is what the person you're calling or texting will see.

How do incoming calls work?

When someone calls one of your Twilio numbers that's set to ring this device (see Incoming in Settings), Dialcrest shows a native incoming-call screen — accept to answer, decline to reject. This works even when the app is closed: a push notification wakes it up to show the call.

Where's my call history?

The Calls tab lists your recent incoming, outgoing, and missed calls, pulled directly from your Twilio account and cached on your device. It's not stored on Dialcrest's servers.

Can I use more than one phone number?

Yes, if your Twilio account has more than one number. In Settings, the Outgoing section picks a single number as your caller ID for calls and texts you send, and the Incoming section lets you check as many numbers as you want to have ring this device.

What does Vacation mode do?

Switching to Vacation in Settings stops incoming calls from ringing on this device — handy when you're away and don't want to be disturbed. It only affects your device: other people or devices using the same Twilio account are unaffected, and you can still see missed calls and messages when you switch back to Online.

Why didn't my phone ring for an incoming call?

A few common causes: the number that was called isn't checked under Incoming in Settings; the device is in Vacation mode; notification permissions were denied; or, on iOS specifically, VoIP push hasn't been configured for this deployment yet (a one-time setup step for whoever runs the app's backend). Outgoing calls and texts aren't affected by any of this.

How does the free trial and subscription work?

Dialcrest starts with a free trial. Afterwards, a subscription (monthly or yearly, purchased through the App Store or Google Play) is required to keep using the app. Your remaining trial days and current plan are shown at the top of Settings. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled through your App Store or Google Play account.

Is Dialcrest affiliated with Twilio? Who do I pay?

Dialcrest is an independent app built on the Twilio Voice SDK and Twilio's APIs — it isn't operated or endorsed by Twilio. There are two separate charges to be aware of: your Twilio account bills you directly for calls, texts, and phone numbers, at Twilio's own rates; your Dialcrest subscription (see above) is a separate charge for the app itself.

What data does Dialcrest store?

Very little, and never your call or message content — that stays with Twilio, under your account. Full details, including exactly what's stored on-device versus in Dialcrest's backend, are in the Privacy Policy.

Where's the source code?

Dialcrest is open source under the GPLv3 license. The full app (Flutter) and backend (Firebase Cloud Functions) source is available on GitHub.
