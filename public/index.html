Your phone number, your Twilio account.

Dialcrest turns your own Twilio number into a full calling and texting app — no carrier lock-in, no middleman reading your traffic. Connect your Twilio credentials and start calling in minutes.

🍎 App Store ▶ Google Play

Coming soon — links will go live at launch.

Bring your own Twilio account

Dialcrest never sees your call or message content. Everything runs through the Twilio account and number you already control.

Calls, texts, and history

A dialer, inbound call handling, SMS/MMS conversations, and call history — the essentials of a phone app, backed by Twilio Voice.

Vacation mode

Step away without missing business — silence incoming calls on your device while other lines on the same Twilio account keep ringing.

Choose your numbers

Pick which Twilio number rings your device, and which one shows up as your caller ID when you call or text out.

Available in 5 languages

English, German, French, Spanish, and Dutch, with more to come.

Open source

The full app and backend source is public under the GPLv3 license — inspect it, self-host it, or contribute.

Open source, by design

Dialcrest is developed in the open. Read the code, file an issue, or run your own build against your own Firebase/Twilio project.
